Big Bedrock — VyOS

Small router/firewall safety checklist for the tryout topology.

DoneAlmost DoneReturn to Complete

Baseline

Record interfaces, assigned addresses, default route, and current NAT/firewall configuration.
Confirm LAN is 172.16.1.0/24 and do not change competition addressing unless directed.

NAT / Public Access

Preserve the configured 1:1 NAT used to expose services on public addresses.
If a scored service disappears externally, verify NAT/firewall before assuming the server itself is broken.

Firewall

Review rules before editing them; avoid broad destructive changes.
Do not block source IP addresses or create scoring-engine-only allow rules.
Keep required public services reachable from the public network.

Recovery

Before a major change, save/record the relevant configuration so you can roll back.
After changes, verify routing/NAT and then validate services from the appropriate side of the network.
